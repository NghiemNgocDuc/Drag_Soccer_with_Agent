Your first match

  1. Choose your teams. Set the lineup and formation before kickoff.
  2. Pick a player and kick. Drag backward, then release. The direction and distance of your drag set the kick.
  3. Read the result. Watch the ball and players move, then plan your next turn.
  4. Play for the win. Score goals and stay ahead. Tied matches can continue into extra time and penalties.

A team that feels like yours

Choose teams, formations, names, and kits. Four player stats affect how the squad moves:

  • Size changes a player's radius.
  • Power changes kick strength.
  • Weight changes mass and collision response.
  • Agility changes how quickly motion settles.
