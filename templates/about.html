Agent Soccer — 2D to 3D, human & AI

Browser-based 2v2–11v11 3D soccer. Human or AI players take turns kicking (slingshot drag). Physics is pymunk (Chipmunk2D) with ground-only ball, FIFA 105×68 field, 8-tier stadium, 2.2k crowd, sun+clouds. Choose from 48 WC2026 teams + 10 formations, 6×2 keeper styles, 4 point-buy stats.

How to play (30 sec)

  1. Pick teams — 2 players cannot pick same team. Team chooser shows crest + manager + formation (default 7 players, 2-3-1). Change formation before kickoff.
  2. Drag & kick — drag your nearest player backward, release. Power = drag distance. Keys: S short, A long, W through, Q switch player, E hold sprint +18%.
  3. Watch — ball rolls (CatmullRom), players lerp eased, referee wanders, crowd cheers on goal.
  4. Win — first to win_goal_limit (5 default) or lead at 90' (extra time 2×15' → penalties). Clock runs 3 real sec = 1 game min.

Views & controls

  • Top view (−1500,790,320) broadcast, Player view behind ball (−260/+260,92,135) follows ball — toggle View (120s timeout, saved).
  • Penalty — kicker view (behind spot) vs keeper view (behind goal), 5s keeper timer, auto-kick.
  • Camera: OrbitControls, `min 300 max 2600`, damping 0.08.
  • Sound: synth WebAudio — kick 0.16s, bounce 0.10s, goal fanfare 1s, whistle 0.8s, crowd ambient+cheer. `` toggles.

Teams & formations

48 WC2026 nations (USA ... Iraq) each with manager (e.g., Brazil Dorival 2-3-1) and 11 names (GK first). Default 7v7, 10 most popular 7v7: 2-3-1, 3-2-1, 2-1-2-1, 3-1-2, 2-2-2, 1-3-2, 3-3-0, 1-4-1, 2-1-3, 1-2-3. Change in team chooser before reset.

Players & keeper

4 stats 20–80 (200 per player): Size→radius 12+stat*16, Power→kick 5+stat*10, Weight→mass 3+stat*4, Agility→friction 1000+stat*1000. Keeper 6×2 PlayStyles: Footwork, Rush Out, Deflector, Cross Claimer, Far Reach, Far Throw (+ stronger). New: per-player custom name (20 chars) + kit color in /customize → overrides team, shows in 3D.

Social — friends, clans, community

  • Friends — 32 cap, presence online/in_match/offline + `last seen`, `+` auto `Model N`, `` favorite, `` nickname (24), `` block, `Recently Met` 30, 1-click `Invite` (password optional).
  • Clans — create `open` (free) or `request` (leader approves), rename, add member by username, shareable link ` /clans/join/<12hex>` 7d, transfer leader (loses role), tournaments with timezone (`8am UTC+7` → UTC, local shows) + `10min` DQ if not joined.
  • Community — share model with `description` + `paper/docs links` (5 max, `http/https`), show `benchmark %` + `likes` + `comments`, others view/like/comment at `/community`.
