    02 / REVIEW BEFORE SAVING

    Find the exact moment.

    Select only the part that matches your movement. Transitions outside this range are context, not neutral examples.

    Leave at least 0.47 seconds of earlier context. Select at least 0.15 seconds. For a jump, include takeoff and airborne motion, then trim the landing.

    WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

    Collect first. Learn second.

    There is no trained personal model yet. Record all five movements across several separate sessions, then download your dataset. More varied, carefully labeled examples help us measure whether the model improves on the current controls.

    The repository's Motion Lab training guide explains how to train with PyTorch on the Unity cluster and export a browser model. Load that model in Metro Motion to compare its predictions before enabling experimental controls. Downloading a dataset does not start a training job.

    Only joint coordinates and random participant/session IDs are saved. These still describe how a person moves: keep your exported files private unless you choose to share them.
